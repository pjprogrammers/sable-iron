Old-world craft.
New-world ease.

No conveyor belts, no rushed buzzers. Just a hot towel, a sharp blade and a barber who listens.

We opened our doors with a simple idea: slow down. A haircut is a small, reliable way to feel like yourself again — so it deserves more than fifteen minutes and a numbered ticket.

Every barber here trained the long way: scissors first, clippers second, conversation always. We keep the music low, the towels hot and the appointments unhurried. You'll get a proper consultation, a drink in your hand and a cut that still looks right three weeks later.
